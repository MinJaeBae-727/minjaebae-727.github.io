Research Experience

Doctoral research, Plant Stress Epigenetics, Konkuk University (Prof. Dae-Jin Yun)
2021 – present

Linker histone H1.3 in the cold response

  • Followed up linker histone H1, previously found among HOS15-associated proteins by IP-MS, and confirmed the HOS15–H1.3 interaction in yeast and N. benthamiana.
  • Showed that cold lowers H1.3 protein in a HOS15-dependent, MG132-sensitive manner (immunoblot and native-promoter H1-GFP lines).
  • Found that h1.3 mutants have higher basal (non-acclimated) freezing tolerance and maintain rosette growth at 4 °C.
  • Integrated NicE-seq, RNA-seq and LC-MS data: h1.3 gains accessibility mainly in pericentromeric regions, and before cold already expresses part of the wild-type cold-induced stress and phenylpropanoid program, while CBF-target COR genes are not pre-induced.

HOS15 self-association and HD2C degradation

  • Showed that HOS15 self-associates through its N-terminal LisH motif; alanine substitutions of conserved hydrophobic LisH residues abolish self-association in yeast two-hybrid assays.
  • Generated complementation lines showing that these LisH variants fail to support cold-induced ubiquitination and degradation of HD2C and do not restore the freezing tolerance of hos15-2.
  • Predicted disordered regions of HOS15 from structure models (AlphaFold-type models and IDR predictors).
